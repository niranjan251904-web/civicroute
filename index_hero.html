How Multi-Department Auto-Splitting Works

Traditional municipal grievance tools assign a single tag (e.g., "Roads") to a complaint containing multiple issues. CivicRoute AI uses natural language understanding (NLU) to identify sub-sentences, mapping distinct clauses to designated nodal officers, calculating independent SLAs for each sub-task, and automatically enforcing district-level oversight.
